# Code of Conduct

Project discussion must be technically rigorous and personally respectful.
Critique claims, designs, evidence, and patches rather than people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of private or
security-sensitive information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, applied
consistently, and offer an appeal path to a maintainer not involved in the
original decision when the maintainer group is large enough.

Before opening the project publicly, replace this interim policy with a named,
versioned community standard and publish a private contact for reports.
